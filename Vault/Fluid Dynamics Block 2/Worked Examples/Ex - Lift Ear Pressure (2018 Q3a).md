# Ex - Lift Ear Pressure (2018 Q3a)

**Uses:** [[Hydrostatic Pressure Distribution]] · [[Compressible Atmosphere]] (why we can ignore it here)

## Problem
Your ears "pop" in a lift as the pressure inside and outside them equalises. Find the pressure change for a lift travelling $\Delta h = 46$ m. Air density $\rho = 1.225\,\text{kg/m}^3$.

## Solution
- Treat $\rho$ as **constant**: 46 m is tiny compared with the scale height of the atmosphere.
- Treat $g = 9.81\,\text{m/s}^2$ as constant ($\Delta h \ll R_{earth}$).

$$\Delta p = -\rho g\,\Delta h = -(1.225)(9.81)(46) = -552.8\,\text{Pa}$$

> [!success] Answer
> Going **up**: $p_1 - p_0 = -0.553\,\text{kPa}$. Going **down**: $p_0 - p_1 = +0.553\,\text{kPa}$.
