# Ex - Pool Drain Hose (P3.59)

**Uses:** [[Free Jets]] · [[Bernoulli Equation]]

## Problem
A 10 m smooth garden hose (inner diameter 20 mm) drains a wading pool. The pool surface is 0.2 m above the ground at the pool; the hose outlet is 0.23 m below that ground level. Neglect viscous effects. Find $Q$.

## Solution
Bernoulli from the pool surface (1) to the outlet (2): $p_1 = p_2 = 0$, $V_1 = 0$, $z_1 = 0.2$ m, $z_2 = -0.23$ m.
$$V_2 = \sqrt{2g(z_1 - z_2)} = \sqrt{2(9.81)(0.43)} = 2.90\,\text{m/s}$$
$$Q = \frac{\pi}{4}(0.020)^2(2.90) = 9.11 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{m}^3/\text{s}$$

> [!success] Answer
> $Q \approx 9.11 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{m}^3/\text{s}$

> [!tip]
> The hose's shape and length don't matter in the ideal model — only the height difference between the free surface and the outlet. (In reality, friction in 10 m of hose would reduce $Q$; see [[Bernoulli Limitations]].)
