# Absolute and Gauge Pressure

Part of [[Fluid Statics]] · used throughout [[Manometry Rules]] and [[Bernoulli Equation]] problems

$$\boxed{p_{gauge} = p_{abs} - p_{atm}}$$

- **Absolute** — measured from absolute zero (perfect vacuum). Never negative.
- **Gauge** — measured from local atmospheric pressure. Can be **positive** (above atmospheric) or **negative** (below, "vacuum").
- **Barometric** — the atmospheric pressure itself, measured with a [[Barometer]].

```
   p_abs ──────────────────  ↑ gauge (+)
   p_atm ══════════════════  ↓ gauge (−)
   p_abs ──────────────────
   0 abs (vacuum) ─────────
```

> [!tip] Exam habit
> Work in **gauge** whenever surfaces are open to air: $p_{atm} = 0$ gauge at free surfaces and [[Free Jets]].
> If atmospheric pressure acts on *both* sides of a gate, it cancels — changing $p_{atm}$ (e.g. to 1.5 atm) changes nothing. See [[Ex - Gate Opening Depth (2023)]] part (b).
> Watch for questions that give **abs** values: e.g. 172 kPa abs = 71 kPa gauge in [[Ex - Impinging Streams]].
