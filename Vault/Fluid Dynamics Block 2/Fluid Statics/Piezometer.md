# Piezometer

Pressure-measuring device · simplest case of the [[Manometry Rules]] · **one fluid only**

A vertical tube open to the atmosphere is attached to a vessel (or pipe). The liquid rises to height $h$ above point A:
$$p_A = p_{atm} + \gamma h \quad\Rightarrow\quad \boxed{p_A = \gamma h \;\;(\text{gauge})}$$

> [!warning] Limitations
> - Only **positive** gauge pressure (a vacuum would suck air in).
> - Liquids only — not gases.
> - High pressures need an impractically **tall column** → use a [[U-Tube Manometer]] instead.

## In flow problems
Piezometer taps on a pipe wall read **static pressure** (see [[Pressure Components in Flow]]). They are how a [[Venturi Meter]] is read, and an open vertical tube on a pipe sets the overflow limit in [[Ex - Overflow Limit (2018 Q4c)]].
