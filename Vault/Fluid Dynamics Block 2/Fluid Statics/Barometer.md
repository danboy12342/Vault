# Barometer

Pressure-measuring device · part of [[Manometry Rules|manometry]] · measures [[Absolute and Gauge Pressure|atmospheric (absolute) pressure]]

An inverted tube of mercury stands in a dish open to the air. The top of the tube is (nearly) a **vacuum**, $p_C \approx 0$ abs.

Points A (dish surface) and B (inside the tube, same level) are in the same fluid at the same height, so $p_A = p_B$:
$$p_A = p_C + \gamma_{Hg}\,h \quad\Rightarrow\quad \boxed{p_{atm} = \gamma_{Hg}\,h}$$

At sea level $h \approx 760\,\text{mm}$ → see [[Pressure Head]].
