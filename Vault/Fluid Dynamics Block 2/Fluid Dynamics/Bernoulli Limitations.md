# Bernoulli Limitations

When the [[Bernoulli Equation]] is **not** valid — any of its four assumptions fails:
1. not steady · 2. not incompressible · 3. not inviscid · 4. not along one streamline (unless the flow is uniform)

## Flow past bodies
- **Fully laminar flow around an airfoil (low Re)** → Bernoulli applies almost everywhere. Top: high $V$, low $p$. Bottom: low $V$, high $p$ → lift.
- **Boundary layer** next to the surface → high friction (viscous) → Bernoulli **not valid** there. See [[Ideal vs Real Fluids]] (no-slip).
- **High angle of attack** → **flow separation**, with wakes or eddies behind the body → Bernoulli **not valid** in the wake.
- **Spinning cylinder** → faster flow on one side → low $p$ there → lift generated.

## In devices
Real flow meters lose energy to friction and eddies → [[Discharge Coefficient]].
