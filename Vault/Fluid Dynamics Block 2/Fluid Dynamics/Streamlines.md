# Streamlines

Part of [[Elementary Fluid Dynamics]] · the path along which the [[Bernoulli Equation]] is applied

- **Streamline**: a line that is **tangent to the velocity vector** everywhere in the flow at an instant — like a photograph of the flow past a cylinder at time $t$.
  $$\frac{dx}{u} = \frac{dy}{v} = \frac{dz}{w}$$
- Fluid **never crosses** a streamline (velocity is along it).
- In **steady** flow, streamlines don't change with time and coincide with particle paths ([[Steady vs Uniform Flow]]).
- **Streamtube**: a thin bundle of streamlines; its walls are streamlines, so it behaves like a pipe with no flow through the sides. Used to derive Bernoulli → [[Bernoulli Derivation - Streamtube]].

## Streamline coordinates
- $\hat{\mathbf s}$ — unit vector **along** the streamline (flow direction)
- $\hat{\mathbf n}$ — unit vector **perpendicular** to the streamline

## Special streamlines
- **Stagnation streamline** — ends on a body at the stagnation point, splitting flow over and under it → [[Stagnation Pressure]].

> [!tip] Picking points in exam problems
> Bernoulli needs two points **on the same streamline**. A common choice is a free surface (known $p$, $V \approx 0$) and an exit or a pipe tap. In channel flow, use the middle streamline ([[Ex - Channel Ramp Flowrate]]).
