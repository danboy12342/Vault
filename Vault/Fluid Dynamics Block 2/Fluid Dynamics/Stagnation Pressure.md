# Stagnation Pressure

From [[Pressure Components in Flow]] · the principle behind the [[Pitot-Static Tube]]

- **Stagnation point $S$** — where the flow is brought to rest on a body: $V_S = 0$.
- **Stagnation streamline** — the [[Streamlines|streamline]] that ends at $S$, dividing the flow that goes over the body from the flow that goes under it.

Bernoulli from a free-stream point A to S on the stagnation streamline ($z_S \approx z_A$, $V_S = 0$):
$$p_A + \tfrac12\rho V_A^2 = p_S$$
$$\boxed{p_s = p + \tfrac12\rho V^2}$$
**Stagnation pressure = static + dynamic.** All the kinetic energy turns into a pressure rise, with no losses.

> [!tip] In problems
> A stagnation point gives you a free "$V = 0$" for Bernoulli, and it is **not** at atmospheric pressure even in an open jet — see [[Ex - Impinging Streams]], where two jets meet at a stagnation point A.
